SaySo

Boss your phone around. Just say so.

Say what you'd like to keep track of - your dog's shots, Mom's recipes, your medicines - and SaySo makes it for you, with the right boxes, dates and reminders. Fill it by talking, typing, or taking a picture of a card. Share it with family. On iPhone Duo and iPad it opens like a book.

Help

Making a keeper from your words uses Apple Intelligence, on the device. If your iPhone doesn't have it turned on, every one of the 30 ready-made keepers still works, and so does everything else.

Sharing with family: open the keeper, tap More, then Share with family. iCloud needs to be on.

Nothing is lost by one tap: things you delete wait in Recently Deleted (Settings) until you put them back.
