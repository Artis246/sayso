JuneBug - Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026 (JuneBug 1.2.1). Developer: Arthur Joyce. JuneBug was previously called SaySo; only the name changed.

Everything you say stays on your phone. From version 1.2, JuneBug never sends your dreams or lists anywhere: no servers of our own, no account, no tracking, no iCloud copying, and nobody at JuneBug ever sees them.

JuneBug does not require an account. We do not collect your personal data, we use no advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you. We have no servers of our own.

What stays on your device

  • Your dreams, your lists and everything in them (words, numbers, dates, photos, checklists and voice notes) are saved on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Making a keeper and reading a card happen on your device, using Apple's on-device intelligence and Apple's on-device text recognition. Your words and photos are not sent to us or to any AI service.
  • Microphone and speech: used only when you tap the microphone, to turn what you say into text on your device, or to record a voice note you choose to keep.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or pick a photo. The app does not browse your photo library.
  • No AI interprets your dreams. Your dream entries are never given to any AI.
  • The little bug that talks back (version 1.2.1) uses pre-written lines and questions, spoken by your device's own voice; you can turn its voice off in Settings. Your answers are added to that dream on your device.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS.

iCloud

From version 1.2, JuneBug no longer copies anything to iCloud and no longer shares keepers with family. A keeper you shared stays on your phone as your own copy. If you used iCloud with an earlier version, an old copy may still be in your own private iCloud; you can delete it on your iPhone in Settings, then your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, JuneBug. If your phone's own iCloud Backup is on, that backup can include JuneBug's data, so a new iPhone can get it back; we cannot access it.

Android

On Android, everything you keep is saved only on your phone. JuneBug for Android has no internet access at all. Speech is turned into words by your phone's own on-device speech recognizer, photos are taken with your camera app or chosen with Android's photo picker, and reminders are scheduled by Android on your phone. There is no iCloud on Android: sharing a keeper sends it as a message with an app you choose. If your phone's own backup is on, Android may include JuneBug's data in your Google backup; we cannot access it.

Keeping and deleting your content

Things you delete wait in Recently Deleted until you put them back. Deleting the app removes what is on that device. Device backups may keep a copy according to your backup settings; we cannot access them.

Email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and message through our email provider and use them only to reply. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps visitors' IP addresses in security logs; we add no analytics or ads.

Children

JuneBug is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
