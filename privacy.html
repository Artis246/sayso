SaySo - Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026. Developer: Arthur Joyce.

SaySo does not require an account. We do not collect your personal data, we use no advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you. We have no servers of our own.

What stays on your device

  • Your keepers and everything in them (words, numbers, dates, photos, checklists and voice notes) are saved on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Making a keeper and reading a card happen on your device, using Apple's on-device intelligence and Apple's on-device text recognition. Your words and photos are not sent to us or to any AI service.
  • Microphone and speech: used only when you tap the microphone, to turn what you say into text on your device, or to record a voice note you choose to keep.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or pick a photo. The app does not browse your photo library.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS.

iCloud and family sharing

If iCloud is turned on, your keepers are also kept in your own private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit), so they appear on your other devices. If you choose Share with family, the keeper is shared through Apple's iCloud sharing with the people you invite; they can see it and add to it. We cannot see your iCloud data or who you share with. You can stop sharing at any time from the keeper's More menu.

Keeping and deleting your content

Things you delete wait in Recently Deleted until you put them back. Deleting the app removes what is on that device; anything in your iCloud stays there until you remove it in your iPhone's iCloud settings. Device backups may keep a copy according to your backup settings; we cannot access them.

Email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and message through our email provider and use them only to reply. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps visitors' IP addresses in security logs; we add no analytics or ads.

Children

SaySo is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
